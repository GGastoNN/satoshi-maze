# Satoshi Maze V5.5 — Sequential Boss Economy

- Campaign progression is now strictly sequential: only the immediate next level can be started.
- Completed campaign levels remain replayable.
- Normal campaign levels are free.
- Only Boss Maze levels 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90 and 100 require payment.
- Boss prices: 10 / 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 20 / 22 / 24 / 28 / 30 sats.
- Total Boss-gate price across the full campaign is 194 sats, matching the previous 97 × 2 sats level model.
- The old Maze Pass is no longer sold.
- Existing Boss purchases and existing Maze Pass ownership are honored, but never bypass sequential progression.
- Old scores and progress are migrated into explicit campaign completion data.
- Payment verification logic, Lightning endpoint flow, wall-impact gameplay and mandatory intro remain unchanged.
- App version: 5.5.0 (Android versionCode 10).
